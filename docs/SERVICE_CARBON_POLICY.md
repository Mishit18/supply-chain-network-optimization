# Service And Carbon Planning Policy

## Purpose

This synthetic policy gives the RAG copilot a management-style lens for service-level and carbon-price scenarios.

## Service-Level Planning

- Service-level constraints represent a maximum warehouse-to-demand distance.
- Tightening service radius should be justified only when customer experience benefits outweigh the additional logistics cost.
- If the model becomes infeasible under a tight radius, management should add candidate warehouses, relax the threshold, or redesign demand zones.

## Carbon Planning

- Carbon-price sweeps are planning scenarios, not a full ESG accounting model.
- The cost-versus-emissions frontier should be used to identify trade-offs, not to claim real-world emissions compliance.
- A higher carbon price may justify a different route mix or warehouse set if the emissions reduction is material.

## Executive Decision Rule

The recommended network should be selected only after reviewing:

- Total cost versus baselines.
- Robustness under demand shocks.
- Service-level feasibility.
- Capacity stress tests.
- Carbon-price sensitivity.
- Rollout risks and model assumptions.
